The Command Palette is Vast's fastest control surface. Open it with `Ctrl/Cmd+K`.

It searches across actions, tabs, workspaces, bookmarks, history, notes, settings, and supported extension contributions.

## Common commands

Examples include:

* create, close, duplicate, or reopen a tab;
* toggle split view;
* switch workspace;
* open Settings;
* open Notes;
* save a page to Reading List;
* add a bookmark;
* copy the current URL or title;
* print;
* open Extensions;
* open Smart Unload;
* open Session Timeline;
* clear History;
* open Labs tools when available.

## Search behavior

The palette supports fuzzy matching rather than exact-only search. Recent and favorite commands can surface faster over time.

## Internal pages

Advanced settings can hide or show Vast internal pages in the palette. Labs-gated pages remain unavailable until their corresponding feature is enabled.
